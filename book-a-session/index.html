Your free pilot experience

From understanding to trying a change.

The experience includes an initial conversation, a personalised report and a short progress review.

SESSION 01 · 45–50 MIN

Explore your pattern

Discuss the behaviour you want to understand, when it happens, what surrounds it and what you have already tried. Together, explore possible behavioural factors and practical strategies.

BETWEEN SESSIONS

Receive your insight report

Receive a personalised summary of the themes explored, possible patterns and suggested actions. Try one or two strategies in your everyday life.

SESSION 02 · 20 MIN

Review your progress

Reconnect to discuss what you tried, what you noticed, what felt difficult and what you might adjust next.

This is a pilot project. Both sessions and the personalised report are complimentary. Session scheduling will be confirmed individually after reviewing your registration.

A note about the service

DepthMynd Labs offers behavioural-science-informed reflection and personal-development support. Sessions explore everyday behaviours and possible influences, then identify practical strategies to try. They do not provide mental-health diagnosis, psychotherapy, medical advice or treatment, and no particular outcome is guaranteed.
