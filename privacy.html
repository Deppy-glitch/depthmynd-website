Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This page explains how DepthMynd Labs handles information submitted through the enquiry form on this website.

Information we collect

If you contact us through the enquiry form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, college or organisation (if provided), the type of enquiry, and the message you choose to send.

How we use the information

We use these details to review and respond to your enquiry, communicate with you about the service or partnership you asked about, and maintain basic records of incoming enquiries.

Form processing

The website enquiry form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form service. Information you submit is transmitted to that service so the enquiry can be delivered to us. Its processing is also subject to the provider’s own privacy terms.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell enquiry information. We may access or disclose it where needed to respond to you, operate the service, protect our rights, or comply with applicable law. We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes or legal obligations.

Your choices

Please avoid sending sensitive personal or health information through the general enquiry form. To request access, correction or deletion of an enquiry you submitted, contact us using the details below, subject to applicable requirements.
