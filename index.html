Why DepthMynd Labs

Make understanding useful in everyday choices, challenges and growth.

Personal development that goes beyond information.

Young people navigate academic expectations, changing relationships, personal decisions and uncertainty about the future. Information can help, but people also need opportunities to reflect, practise and apply useful skills.

DepthMynd Labs brings behavioural-science principles into practical learning experiences that help students explore their patterns, build skills and identify meaningful next steps.

About us

Making behavioural science useful for young people’s futures.

DepthMynd Labs is a behavioural-science initiative focused on personal development and the skills people use to navigate everyday life.

We run practical learning experiences for young people and work with colleges on ways to build behavioural and emotional skills.

We want self-understanding to be useful in everyday decisions, relationships and goals.
